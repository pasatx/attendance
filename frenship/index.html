Frenship ISD School Finder
Enter your home address or click anywhere on the map
to find your zoned elementary, middle, and high school.
To Find Your School Zones:
1
Enter your address or click the map

Either enter your address in the search bar or click your home on the map.

2
View your zoned campuses

A list of your attendance zones will appear on the left under "Your Zoned Campuses".

3
Scroll for more details

Scroll through the results for additional information on each school.

4
Clear your search

To clear your search, click the "Clear" button (just below the Search button) on the left.

+

Expand Map Controls (above "Your Zoned Campuses") to control map elements including background, attendance zones, and schools.

*PASA cannot guarantee the accuracy of the address search results. Please verify that the results accurately identify your home's location before proceeding. Additionally, if your home is less than a year old, your address might not be in the system yet.

**To obtain your official school district information, such as whether you reside in Frenship ISD or another district, please use the property search tool available on your Lubbock County Appraisal District's website. Note: This mapping tool should not be used to make decisions related to home purchases or rental agreements.
